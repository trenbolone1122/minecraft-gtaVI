# landmark module

Landmark positions, footprints, names and reference photos come from the GTADB community map
(https://gtadb.org, https://github.com/rolux/gtadb.org), including the YANIS v17 map by aiwe, Martipk and
RickRick, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Building footprints, shoreline
and landmark coordinates were read from its data files; the builds are our own block-by-block interpretation.

Trailer frames were used only as visual reference for proportions, colours and furnishing; no images or
assets from the trailers are shipped in the mod.
