## character

All character textures (Jason and Lucia outfits, pedestrian, police, SWAT and npc skins, the ponytail and
cap brim accessory textures and the wardrobe block textures) are original pixel art made for this mod. They are
generated by the scripts in `tools/character/` (Python 3 and Pillow) and contain no copied third-party images.
The designs are fan interpretations of the characters, drawn in Minecraft style; no logos or trademarks are used.
The 3D accessory models and all code in `dev.leonida.character` are original.
