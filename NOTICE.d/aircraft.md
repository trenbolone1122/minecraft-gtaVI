# Aircraft module

The `dev.leonida.aircraft` package contains code and assets adapted from
Immersive Aircraft by Luke100000 (Conczin), licensed under the GNU General
Public License v3.0: https://github.com/Luke100000/ImmersiveAircraft

Adapted parts:

- The flight model: lift, drag, engine thrust, gravity and rotor physics for
  airplanes and helicopters (`entity/AircraftEntity`, `AircraftSpec`).
- The Blockbench `.bbmodel` loader and renderer, including bone pivots,
  per-face UVs and the deferred render buffer (`client/bb/*`).
- Sounds from the Immersive Aircraft 1.7.0 (26.2) jar: `engine_start`,
  `propeller`, `propeller_small`, `propeller_tiny` and `woosh`
  (`assets/leonida/sounds/aircraft/`).

The aircraft models (Western Duster, Floatplane, Mammoth Dodo, Buckingham
Shamal, Buckingham Maverick, Police Maverick, Weazel News Maverick, Nagasaki
Buzzard) and the parachute are original, generated by
`src/client/java/dev/leonida/aircraft/models/gen_models.py`.

Changes from upstream: ported to Fabric and Minecraft Java 26.3, new aircraft
roster and models, paint variants, translucent glass and full bright lamps,
crop duster smoke, police searchlight helicopters, parachutes and skydiving.
