# vicecity module

Landmark placement, footprints and names come from the GTADB.org community map (GTADB.org community map, YANIS v17, aiwe, Martipk, RickRick), licensed CC BY 4.0. Footprint polygons in `data/leonida/vicecity/footprints.json` are derived from that map.

Landmark massing reuses dimensions and placement from the project's own explorer pipeline (authored-stadiums.js, authored-hero-landmarks.js, authored-civic-landmarks.js, ocean-beach-frontage.js). Real-world proportions follow public descriptions of the reference buildings. All geometry, textures and lettering are original; no studio logos are reproduced.
