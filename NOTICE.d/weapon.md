## Weapon module (dev.leonida.weapon)

- Just Enough Guns by MigaMi, with credits to MrCrayfish, Bomb787 and NineZero
  (https://github.com/MigaMiDev/Just-Enough-Guns), GPL. Gun stat tuning (damage, fire rate, magazine,
  reload, spread, recoil) started from its gun definitions, and the hitscan, pellet damage pooling,
  invulnerability-frame reset and damage falloff approach is adapted from it for Fabric 26.3.
- Sound files in `assets/leonida/sounds/weapon/` are taken from Just Enough Guns, which ships them under
  Creative Commons 0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) per its `sounds/SOUND-LICENSE.txt`.
- Gun models, textures, icons and the weapon wheel art are our own, generated by
  `src/main/java/dev/leonida/weapon/assetgen/gen_assets.py`.
