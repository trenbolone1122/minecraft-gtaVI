# World module

The Leonida terrain, roads, water, building footprints and place names are derived from the
YANIS community map of Leonida, YANIS / map.gtadb.org, licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: the map art was classified into surface
classes, building footprints were traced, heights were estimated from the elevation bands, and the
result was resampled to 1 block per metre for this mod. Place names come from the same community
landmark data set.
